Concept Sketchbook

    See the shape of a subject before you learn it.

    Type a topic and an AI sketches a rough concept map in pencil. It is a draft, not an authority: guess first, check against a source, doubt what looks wrong, and add what you know.

    1. Guess. Write your own one-line explanation before you see the draft.
    2. Check or doubt. Use the search links, then mark a concept checked, or doubt it or a single link.
    3. Add. Your own concepts are diamonds, so you can see what you know and what the AI guessed.

    You need an OpenRouter key (a free one works). Free models may train on prompts, so don't type private material. Maps stay in this browser.

      Settings

      OpenRouter key

      Kept for this tab only and cleared when you close it. Never saved in maps or exports, never put in a URL, and sent only to openrouter.ai. Use a key with a low credit limit.

      Models

      Free models allow about 50 requests a day (1,000 after buying $10 of OpenRouter credits). A map costs 4 to 8 calls.

      Learning

      Free models may train on what you send. Prompts contain only your topic and the app's instructions; don't type private material.
